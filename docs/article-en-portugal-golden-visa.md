## Article

The Portuguese golden visa is the most written-about residence-by-investment route in Europe and one of the most inaccurately described. Three years after the property option was repealed, pages selling it still print property thresholds. Sixteen months after the naturalisation period doubled, the largest investment-migration site on the internet still describes the route as ending in citizenship on the old timetable.

This page sets out what article 3(1) of Lei 23/2007 actually offers today, what article 90-A does and does not waive, what the permit costs beyond the investment, and where the route now ends. Every figure carries the instrument that sets it and the date that instrument was read.

### Portugal golden visa requirements in 2026: the five routes that remain

The programme is not a scheme with its own statute. It is a residence permit for investment activity under **article 90-A of Lei 23/2007**, and the qualifying activities are the list in **article 3(1)(d)** of the same law. That list once had eight entries. It now has five.

![1]

| Subparagraph | What it asks | Status |
| --- | --- | --- |
| i | Capital transfer of €1.5m | Repealed 2023 |
| ii | Ten jobs created | In force |
| iii | Property purchase | Repealed 2023 |
| iv | Property purchase plus rehabilitation | Repealed 2023 |
| v | €500,000 into research | In force |
| vi | €250,000 into cultural heritage | In force |
| vii | €500,000 into a fund | In force |
| viii | €500,000 into a company creating five jobs | In force |

**Article 53 of Lei 56/2023**, the housing law known as *Mais Habitação*, repealed subparagraphs i, iii and iv: both property options and the capital transfer, in one provision.

The part that gets missed is **article 3(5)** of the same law, which separately prohibits an investment channelled **directly or indirectly** into real estate. So the workaround that circulates — subscribe to a fund that happens to hold housing — is closed by a different provision from the one that closed the property route, and closing only the first would not have closed the second.

Two of the surviving routes are rarely listed anywhere. Subparagraph ii asks for **ten jobs created, eight in low-density areas, with no capital threshold at all**: there is no sum of money attached to it. Subparagraph vi asks **€250,000 into cultural heritage, €220,000 in low-density areas** — half the price of the fund route, and it appears on almost no comparison page.

### Is the Portugal golden visa suspended? No, and the confusion has a source

The route is open. Applications are accepted, permits are issued, and the queue is long enough that people reasonably conclude otherwise.

What happened is that three of eight qualifying activities disappeared on one day in 2023, and those three were the ones every advertisement described. A reader who knew the programme as "buy a property in Portugal and get residency" met a route that no longer existed and read that as the programme being suspended. It was not suspended. It was reduced.

#### The €350,000 and €280,000 tiers: what happened to them

Searches for these figures are rising, and the honest answer is short. **No property tier of any amount survives.** The reduced thresholds that circulate belonged to the property subparagraphs, and article 53 of Lei 56/2023 repealed those subparagraphs entirely rather than adjusting the amounts inside them. There is no lower tier to find, in any region, for any category of building.

We have not read the historical amounts in a primary source and do not restate them as figures of ours. What we have read is the repeal, and the repeal is what settles the question.

### Portugal golden visa investment fund: €500,000, and three conditions on the fund itself

Subparagraph vii is the route most applicants now take, and the money is the least of its conditions.

- **€500,000** subscribed to units of a fund or venture capital fund.
- The fund **may not be a real-estate fund**, which follows from article 3(5) as much as from the subparagraph.
- The term must be **at least five years**.
- At least **60% of the fund's assets** must sit in companies with their registered seat in Portugal.

The last condition is the one that disqualifies products in practice. A pan-European fund with a Portuguese sleeve does not meet it, and the 60% test is on the fund, not on the investor's share of it.

### Does the Portugal golden visa have an income requirement? Yes, and here is the article

This is the claim most likely to contradict what you have read elsewhere, so it is set out with its provenance.

**Article 90-A(1)(a)** requires that applicants *"Preencham os requisitos gerais estabelecidos no artigo 77.º, com exceção da alínea a) do n.º 1"*: meet the general requirements of article 77, **with the exception of subparagraph (a) of paragraph 1**.

**Article 77(1)** lists those requirements. Subparagraph (a) is possession of a valid residence visa. Subparagraph (d) is *"Posse de meios de subsistência"* — possession of means of subsistence, as defined by the order referred to in article 52(1)(d). That order is **Portaria 1563/2007**, the same one that sets the D7 scale.

The exception is singular and it is named. It removes the visa. It does not remove the income test.

| Requirement of art. 77(1) | Investment permit |
| --- | --- |
| (a) valid residence visa | Waived by art. 90-A(1)(a) |
| (d) means of subsistence | Applies |
| (e) accommodation | Applies |
| (g) absence of relevant convictions | Applies |

The amount is the national minimum wage: **€920 a month** since 1 January 2026, under article 3 of Decreto-Lei 139/2025, with **50% for a second adult and 30% per child** under article 2(2) of Portaria 1563/2007.

The practical consequence is small in money and large in paperwork. An investor who has moved half a million euros will clear €920. But it is a document to produce, and a page that tells you the investment route has no income requirement has told you something that is not in the law.

**How widely is it missed?** On 6 September 2026 we read the two largest guides ranking for this term. Neither contains the words *means of subsistence*. One, at 57,000 characters, mentions proof of income only in a paragraph about opening a bank account. This is not a subtlety buried in a schedule; it is the second requirement in the article that creates the permit.

### Portugal golden visa processing time: 60 days in the statute, one to three years in the queue

**Article 82 of Lei 23/2007** gives 60 days to decide a grant and 30 days to decide a renewal, with tacit approval on a renewal where the delay is not the applicant's fault.

The queue does not. Submission to biometrics runs 6 to 24 months; biometrics to card runs a further 6 to 18. As of 4 August 2026, AIMA reported roughly **30,000 pending files**. Any page promising six to nine months is quoting the law and calling it the timetable.

The gap matters beyond patience, because the clock that leads to citizenship starts at the **issue** of the permit, not at the application. Two years of queue are two years that do not count.

Add a lawyer and the fund's own subscription and management commissions and the real range is **€30,000–50,000 for one applicant and €65,000–90,000 for a family** — figures we give as a range because the legal and commission components are market prices, not published ones, and we do not present a market price as a statutory one.

### Portugal golden visa to citizenship: ten years since 19 May 2026, not five

**Lei Orgânica 1/2026 of 18 May**, in force **19 May 2026**, rewrote article 6(1)(b) of Lei 37/81. The naturalisation period is now:

- **seven years** for nationals of EU member states and of Portuguese-speaking countries;
- **ten years** for everybody else.

Against five years for everybody before. The law also added an examination in culture, history and national symbols, and a solemn declaration of adherence to the democratic rule of law.

**The transitional rule is article 7(2):** proceedings already pending on 19 May 2026 are decided under the previous text. It is the pending application that is protected, not the pending residence.

And the period runs from the **issue** of the residence permit. On a route where the card can take one to three years to arrive, that is the difference between a ten-year plan and a thirteen-year one.

This is the single fact most often missing from pages selling the route. On 6 September 2026 the largest investment-migration site's Portugal golden visa page did not mention the change at all; the second still described renewable two-year permits without naming where the road ends.

### Family reunification on a golden visa: the two-year wait does not apply

**Lei 61/2025 of 22 October** made family reunification conditional on already holding a residence permit valid for at least two years, and rewrote who counts as family.

Holders under **articles 90, 90-A and 121-A keep their exemption** from that wait, and article 90-A is the investment permit. So the two-year rule that now governs most Portuguese routes does not govern this one — one of the few places where the investment permit is treated more favourably than an ordinary permit, and it survived a law that tightened almost everything else.

Dependent minors and incapacitated persons keep the exemption in every case.

### Figures in circulation with no legal source

- **"€350,000 rehabilitation tier."** No property tier survives. See above.
- **"Six to nine months."** That is article 82's 60-day grant deadline, rounded upward and presented as a queue time.
- **"No income requirement."** Article 90-A(1)(a) names one exception and it is the visa.
- **"Five years to citizenship."** Correct until 18 May 2026 and wrong since.
- **"€250,000 arts route is closed."** Subparagraph vi is in force at €250,000, €220,000 in low-density areas.

### Frequently asked questions

? Can I still buy property for a Portuguese golden visa?
No. Article 53 of Lei 56/2023 repealed both property subparagraphs in 2023, and article 3(5) separately prohibits investment channelled indirectly into real estate, which closes the fund workaround as well.

? Is the Portugal golden visa suspended in 2026?
No. It is open and applications are being decided. Three of the eight qualifying activities were repealed in 2023, and those three were the ones most advertised.

? Do I have to prove income for the Portugal golden visa?
Yes. Article 90-A(1)(a) waives only subparagraph (a) of article 77(1), the residence visa. Subparagraph (d), means of subsistence, applies — €920 a month for the main applicant, 50% for a second adult, 30% per child.

? How long does the Portugal golden visa take?
Article 82 gives 60 days for a decision on a grant. In practice one to three years from filing to card, against roughly 30,000 pending files as of 4 August 2026.

? How much does the Portugal golden visa cost beyond the investment?
About €13,470 in AIMA fees for one applicant to the first renewal, about €40,400 for a family of three. With legal fees and fund commissions, €30,000–50,000 for one and €65,000–90,000 for a family.

? How many years to citizenship on a Portuguese golden visa?
Ten, or seven for EU and Portuguese-speaking-country nationals, since 19 May 2026. Applications already pending on that date are decided under the old five-year rule. The clock starts when the permit is issued, not when it is applied for.

? Do I have to live in Portugal on a golden visa?
The permit is a residence permit, and the stay requirement is what makes it usable at a distance rather than a formality to ignore. What the permit does not do is accumulate the citizenship period while it sits unissued in a queue.

? Can I bring my family immediately?
Yes. Lei 61/2025 imposed a two-year wait on most routes and expressly preserved the exemption for holders under articles 90, 90-A and 121-A.
