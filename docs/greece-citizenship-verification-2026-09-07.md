# Greek citizenship: verification, 7 September 2026

Read for B3 (the naturalisation clock) and as the prerequisite for C4. Source:
**the Ministry of the Interior's own consolidated text of Law 3284/2004, the
Greek Citizenship Code, consolidated to 17 September 2025**, published at
https://www.ypes.gr/kodikas-ellinikis-ithageneias/ with a downloadable unified
text at https://www.ypes.gr/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/20250917-KEI-NOMOS.pdf

The consolidation is by NOMOS / Netcompany-Intrasoft under licence, and the
ministry's own header says it is informational and subject to checking against
the Government Gazette. **Status: OFFICIAL host, CONSOLIDATION by a licensed
third party.** Not the Gazette itself.

## The structure, which we had wrong in one respect

Naturalisation is not one article. The Code splits it:

- **Άρθρο 5 — Τυπικές προϋποθέσεις** (formal conditions)
- **Άρθρο 5Α — Ουσιαστικές προϋποθέσεις** (substantive conditions) and the
  delegating provision
- **Άρθρο 5Β — Λόγοι ασφαλείας** (security grounds)
- Άρθρο 6 — supporting documents; Άρθρο 7 — procedure; Άρθρο 7Β — referral;
  Άρθρο 8 — decision and reasons; Άρθρο 9 — oath; Άρθρο 10 — ομογενείς abroad.

Our pages cite "art. 5(1)(δ)" for the seven years. **That is correct** — but the
language and history examination is **Άρθρο 5Α**, not article 5, and we have not
been citing it at all.

## Άρθρο 5 §1, verbatim as far as read

Opening: «Ο αλλοδαπός που επιθυμεί να γίνει Έλληνας πολίτης με πολιτογράφηση
πρέπει:»

- **α.** «Να είναι ενήλικος κατά το χρόνο της υποβολής της δήλωσης
  πολιτογράφησης.»
- **β.** «Να μην έχει καταδικασθεί αμετάκλητα για αδίκημα που τέλεσε εκ δόλου,
  κατά την τελευταία δεκαετία …» — a suspended sentence still counts, per the
  sentence that follows.
- **γ.** «Να μην τελεί υπό απέλαση ή άλλη εκκρεμότητα του καθεστώτος νόμιμης
  παραμονής του στη Χώρα.»
- **δ.** «Να διαμένει στην Ελλάδα νόμιμα για **επτά συνεχή έτη** πριν από την
  υποβολή της αίτησης πολιτογράφηση[ς]»
- **ε.** «Να κατέχει έναν από τους παρακάτω τίτλους νόμιμης διαμονής:» followed
  by an **exhaustive list αα) to αι)**.

**§3:** «Ο αλλοδαπός που κατέχει κάθε έγκυρο τίτλο διαμονής, **εκτός των
προσωρινών**, και πληροί τις τυπικές …» — temporary titles are excluded.

## THE POINT FOR OUR READER: the investor permit is named in the list

**Άρθρο 5 §1(ε)(αθ):** «άδεια διαμονής για επενδυτική δραστηριότητα σύμφωνα με
τις διατάξεις **του άρθρου 16 του ν. 4251/2014**»

Two things follow.

**First, and it settles a question we have answered loosely until now:** the
golden-visa permit is a qualifying title for naturalisation, named in the Code
itself rather than inferred. Our `/sources` line already said the investor permit
qualifies; it is now sourced to the provision that says so.

**Second, an open lead for C4, and it must not be published until checked.** The
list points at **article 16 of Law 4251/2014**, the old Migration Code, which
Law 5038/2023 replaced — the investor permit is now article 100 of the new Code.
The consolidation is dated 17 September 2025, well after 5038/2023, and still
carries the old citation.

**This may be a dangling cross-reference of exactly the kind found in the
Portuguese D8 regulation the same day — or it may be nothing**, because Greek
codifying statutes commonly carry a provision that references to the repealed
law are read as references to the corresponding provisions of the new one.
**That provision has not been found or excluded.** Law 5038/2023's final and
repealing articles were not reached: forin.gr's page stops at article 112,
kodiko.gr renders no text, and the Gazette PDF was not located.

**Do not write that the Code points at a repealed article until Law 5038/2023's
reference and repeal provisions have been read.** This is the same check that was
skipped on the D8 and cost six files.

## Άρθρο 5Α — the substantive conditions, and the examination

«Ο αλλοδαπός … πρέπει, πέραν των προϋποθέσεων [του άρθρου 5]:»

- **α.** «Να γνωρίζει επαρκώς την ελληνική γλώσσα, ώστε να εκπληρώνει τις
  υποχρεώσεις που απορρέουν από την …»
- **β.** «Να γνωρίζει επαρκώς την ελληνική ιστορία και γεωγραφία, τον ελληνικό
  πολιτισμό και τις συνήθειες …»
- **γ.** «(Καταργείται).» — repealed.
- **δ.** «Να έχει ενταχθεί ομαλά στην οικονομική και κοινωνική ζωή της Χώρας …»,
  with the following sentence naming what is weighed for economic integration:
  «η ύπαρξη σταθερής εργασίας …»
- **§2** delegates to a decision of the Minister of the Interior the detailed
  evidence of economic and social integration.

**Note the shape, because it is the same shape as Portugal's:** the statute says
"sufficiently" and delegates the level. Our existing note that the ΠΕΓΠ tests
Greek at **B1** plus history, geography and culture with a **70%** pass mark
remains **tier B/C — it is not in the Code**, and on today's reading the Code
names no level at all. Same caution as Portugal's A2.

## The historic figure, for the record

The **original 2004 text** required «δέκα συνολικά έτη την τελευταία δωδεκαετία»
— ten years within the last twelve, five for stateless persons and recognised
refugees, and five for Olympic athletes. That text is superseded and is what a
stale page will be quoting if it says ten or twelve.
