# Portugal D8: verification, 7 September 2026

Read on 7 September 2026, after the D8 income correction earlier the same day.
Everything below is quoted. Source status is marked on each item, because half
of it is a consolidation rather than the gazette.

## 1. The D8 is two visas, and the statute keeps them in different chapters

**Temporary-stay visa — art. 54.º n.º 1 al. i) of Lei 23/2007**, inserted by Lei
18/2022. SECONDARY: LEGISPÉDIA consolidation.

> "Exercício de atividade profissional subordinada ou independente, prestada, de
> forma remota, a pessoa singular ou coletiva com domicílio ou sede fora do
> território nacional"

Art. 54.º n.º 1 opens the whole list with visas "por períodos inferiores a um
ano"; n.º 2 issues the visa for the duration of the stay and allows multiple
entries.

**Residence visa — art. 61.º-B**, inserted by the same law. SECONDARY, same
source. A single paragraph:

> "É concedido a trabalhadores subordinados e profissionais independentes visto
> de residência para o exercício de atividade profissional prestada, de forma
> remota, a pessoas singulares ou coletivas com domicílio ou sede fora do
> território nacional, devendo ser demonstrado o vínculo laboral ou a prestação
> de serviços, consoante o caso."

The two texts are near-identical in what they describe and entirely different in
what they lead to. **Neither states an income figure.**

## 2. The figure is in the regulation, in two places, identically

Arts. 18.º-B and 31.º-A live in **Decreto Regulamentar 84/2007**, inserted there
by **Decreto Regulamentar 4/2022, de 30 de setembro**. PRIMARY: the DRE PDF.
https://files.dre.pt/1s/2022/09/19000/0002800097.pdf

Art. 18.º-B, for the temporary-stay visa, and art. 31.º-A n.º 1, for the
residence visa, both close with the same alínea c):

> "Comprovativo de rendimentos médios mensais auferidos no exercício de
> atividade profissional subordinada ou independente nos últimos três meses de
> valor mínimo equivalente a quatro remunerações mínimas mensais garantidas"

Four RMMG, averaged over the last three months. RMMG 2026 = €920 (Decreto-Lei
139/2025, art. 3.º), so €3,680 — for **both** variants, which is why the market's
single euro figure conceals that there are two visas behind it.

**Cite it as art. 18.º-B / 31.º-A of DR 84/2007, as inserted by DR 4/2022.** The
articles are not standalone provisions of DR 4/2022, and our 7 September
correction phrased it loosely.

## 3. THE FINDING: the regulation's in-country route points at repealed law

Art. 31.º-A **n.º 2**, verbatim (PRIMARY, same DRE PDF; confirmed unchanged
against the LEGISPÉDIA consolidation on 7 September 2026):

> "Quando o requerente não disponha de visto de residência para prestação de
> trabalho remoto, é aplicável, com as necessárias adaptações, o procedimento
> definido no n.º 2 e seguintes dos artigos 88.º e 89.º da Lei n.º 23/2007, de 4
> de julho, na sua redação atual, sendo para esse efeito exigível o cumprimento
> do disposto no número anterior."

That is the only route the D8 regime names for someone already in Portugal
without the visa. It was the manifestação de interesse.

**Decreto-Lei n.º 37-A/2024, de 3 de junho, art. 2.º — norma revogatória.**
REPRODUCTION: read at pgdlisboa; the DRE PDF was not reachable. Verbatim:

> "São revogados os n.os 6 e 7 do artigo 81.º, os n.os 2 e 6 do artigo 88.º e os
> n.os 2, 4 e 5 do artigo 89.º da Lei n.º 23/2007, de 4 de julho, na sua redação
> atual."

Art. 3.º: in force the day after publication — **4 June 2024** — and not applied
to proceedings already begun.

**So "o n.º 2 e seguintes dos artigos 88.º e 89.º" today means:** 88.º n.º 2
repealed, 88.º n.º 6 repealed, 89.º n.º 2 repealed, 89.º n.º 4 repealed, 89.º
n.º 5 repealed. What is left under that heading is 89.º n.º 3 — a holder of an
independent-activity permit may take employed work, which presupposes the permit
the applicant is trying to obtain — and a **new** 89.º n.º 4 given by Lei
61/2025 (PRIMARY, DRE PDF), which is about an entrepreneurial project in a
certified incubator and has nothing to do with remote work.

**The chronology, and it is the whole argument:**

| Date | Instrument | What it did |
|---|---|---|
| 30 Sep 2022 | DR 4/2022 | Inserted arts. 18.º-B and 31.º-A, including the cross-reference |
| 17 Jan 2024 | DR 1/2024 | Seventh and **latest** amendment to DR 84/2007; did not touch either article |
| 4 Jun 2024 | DL 37-A/2024, art. 2.º | Repealed arts. 88(2), 88(6), 89(2), 89(4), 89(5) |
| 8 Nov 2024 | Lei 40/2024 | Widened the transitional protection; restored nothing prospectively |
| 23 Oct 2025 | Lei 61/2025 | Gave art. 89 a new n.º 4 on incubators; added art. 122(1)(s) |

The amendment list for DR 84/2007 is SECONDARY (LEGISPÉDIA): 2/2013, DL 31/2014,
15-A/2015, 9/2018, Lei 71/2018, 4/2022, **1/2024**. Nothing after January 2024.
The repeal is June 2024. The regulation has therefore named a repealed procedure
for two years and three months.

**Lei n.º 40/2024, de 7 de novembro.** REPRODUCTION: pgdlisboa. It does not
restore the route. It excludes from DL 37-A/2024 the proceedings begun before it
and the cases of people already registered and contributing to social security
before its entry into force: *"Os casos referidos no número anterior continuam a
reger-se pela Lei n.º 23/2007, de 4 de julho, na sua redação anterior."*

## 4. And art. 122.º does not catch the remote worker either

Art. 122.º n.º 1 is the general list of residence permits granted **with
exemption from the residence visa**. SECONDARY: LEGISPÉDIA. Nineteen alíneas,
a) to s), enumerated in full on 7 September 2026: minors born in Portugal;
minors in pre-school; adult children of residents; adults born in Portugal;
minors under tutorship; former asylum beneficiaries; people needing prolonged
medical assistance; people who served in the Portuguese armed forces; people who
lost Portuguese nationality; people whose residence right lapsed; parents of
minors resident in Portugal; diplomatic and consular agents; victims of serious
labour offences; holders of a permit under art. 109.º; former secondary-school
students under art. 92.º; former higher-education students under art. 91.º;
**holders of a temporary-stay visa for research or highly qualified activity**
under al. q); investors under al. r); and children in institutional care under
al. s), added by Lei 61/2025.

**Al. q) is the neighbouring temporary-stay visa — art. 54(1)(d) — and not the
remote-work one at art. 54(1)(i).** This is a form-A negative claim: the article
enumerates, the enumeration is complete, and the adjacent category is present
while this one is not.

Art. 122.º n.º 3 applies arts. 88.º, 89.º or 90.º "com a devida adaptação" to
alíneas n), o), p) and q) — sending those categories to the same articles whose
operative paragraphs were repealed.

**What the article may therefore say, and no more than this:** the D8 regime
names no working in-country route. The temporary-stay D8 does not appear in the
conversion list, and the residence D8's own regulation points at repealed
paragraphs. **What it may not say:** that conversion is impossible in practice.
AIMA can run an administrative practice on a general power, we did not reach
AIMA's own checklists, and the Lei 40/2024 transitional protection still covers
people registered before 4 June 2024. The claim is about the published rules.

## 5. What the first results page said on 7 September 2026

Query `portugal digital nomad visa`, US locale. The shape is
**country/route-specific** — our winnable form, no generic listicle at the top.

- **vistos.mne.gov.pt**, the Portuguese government's own visa portal, ranks
  first, and describes only the temporary-stay variant: "Digital Nomad Temporary
  stay visa for the exercise of a professional activity done remotely".
- **Get Golden Visa** — "offers 1-year residency for remote workers", which
  merges the two visas into one and calls the result residency.
- **imin-portugal** — "at least €3,680", printed flat, no year, no multiplier.
- **Allianz Care** — "at least €2,800 per month". That is four times the 2022
  minimum wage. Four years stale, and stale in the direction that gets a file
  refused.
- **Reddit**, top answer — "€3,680 a month and you need to be in Portugal for 6
  consecutive or 8 non-consecutive months to keep your permit".
- **Lexidy**, a law firm, has "Residence Visa vs. Temporary Visa" as a chapter
  of a **YouTube video**. So the two-variant distinction exists in this market,
  in a video, not on a page. **We must not claim to be first to notice it.** What
  no page has is what follows from it.

Nobody names DR 84/2007, art. 18.º-B, art. 31.º-A, DL 37-A/2024, or the
cross-reference.
