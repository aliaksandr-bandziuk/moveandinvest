# Greece: law 5307/2026 read, and article 100Α found

Read 5 September 2026. Companion to `greece-5275-research-2026-09-05.md` and
`greece-verification-2026-08-28.md`.

## Why this was opened

Four Greek pages on the site rested on the Migration Code as it stood in
February 2026. Consolidated texts had begun to appear as "κωδικοποιημένος με
5307/2026", which meant a law existed between our verification and current law
and had not been read. Twice before, exactly this gap had produced a published
error — the Portuguese "90 days" and the Greek "no statutory issuing deadline".

## Finding 1 — law 5307/2026 changes nothing on this site

**Law 5307/2026, ΦΕΚ Α΄ 90 of 11 June 2026**, "Εφαρμογή του Συμφώνου για τη
Μετανάστευση και το Άσυλο, λοιπές διατάξεις του Υπουργείου Μετανάστευσης και
Ασύλου και άλλες επείγουσες διατάξεις". It implements the EU Pact: screening,
reception, unaccompanied minors, the common procedure, returns, qualification,
temporary protection, solidarity, Eurodac.

Its table of contents was walked in full — all 265 articles, article 1 to
article 265, on a Greek legal database. **Exactly one article touches the
Migration Code:**

- **Article 179**, "Προϋποθέσεις απόκτησης του καθεστώτος επί μακρόν διαμένοντος
  – Τροποποίηση άρθρου 144 Κώδικα Μετανάστευσης".

Its operative text was read in the Ministry of Migration and Asylum's published
bill for the same law (ΜΕΡΟΣ ΣΤ΄, articles 160-180). It does three things:

- in the opening sentence of §1, «πολίτη» is replaced by «υπήκοο» — a
  terminological change with no substantive effect;
- the third sentence of §2 is replaced, so that for a person granted
  international protection the period between lodging the application and the
  issue of the permit under art. 24 of Regulation (EU) 2024/1347 counts towards
  the five years;
- a §2Α is added: where a beneficiary of international protection is found in
  another member state without a right to stay there, prior lawful residence in
  Greece does not count.

**Article 144 §1 as it now stands still requires five years of lawful and
uninterrupted residence in Greece immediately before the application.** That is
what this site publishes, including the presence argument in the passport piece.

Articles 95, 100, 100Α, 79Α, 160, 161, 163 and the two-month deadline of art.
100 §10 are untouched by 5307/2026.

Tier: B for the table of contents (commercial legal database, complete and
consistent with a second one), A-minus for art. 179's wording (the ministry's
own published bill, not the gazette page).

## Finding 2 — the €250,000 startup permit exists, and we said it did not

This is the material finding, and it is a correction to our own page.

**Article 100Α of the Migration Code**, "Επενδύσεις σε νεοφυείς επιχειρήσεις
(άδεια διαμονής τύπου «Β.6»)", added by **article 44 of Law 5162/2024**, ΦΕΚ Α΄
198 of 5 December 2024. Twelve paragraphs, read in full in the Ministry of
National Economy and Finance's published bill, where it is numbered article 41.

| § | What it says |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | €250,000 into the capital of an enterprise on the National Registry of Startup Enterprises ("Elevate Greece"), as shares in a capital increase or bonds on a bond issue. Permit granted by the Minister of Migration after certification by the Directorate for Foreign Direct Investment of the Ministry of Development |
| 2 | Cumulative conditions: (a) the holding may not exceed 33% of capital or voting rights; (b) two new jobs within the first year; (c) that headcount maintained five years |
| 3 | Investment through a legal person: domestic, where the applicant holds all its shares; foreign, on the corresponding condition |
| 4 | Funds remitted from abroad. Spouse, partner or relative to the second degree may be the sender |
| 5 | Where art. 94 is not satisfied, the entry application goes to the consulate within the year following the investment; the consulate forwards it within one month to the FDI Directorate |
| 6 | How realisation and holding are certified for registered and for traded securities |
| 7 | Permit of one year, renewed two years at a time while holding is certified. Absences from the country are no bar to renewal. Renewal survives the company's removal from the registry for age. Competent: the Residence Permits Directorate |
| 8 | The card reads «Β.6 Άδεια διαμονής για πραγματοποίηση επένδυσης σε νεοφυή επιχείρηση» |
| 9 | No right of access to any form of employment — the same words as art. 100 §9 |
| 10 | Shares held five years. Afterwards the permit survives a sale if new qualifying shares, or an investment under arts. 96-100, follow within two months. Bankruptcy or corporate transformation: two months to reinvest |
| 11 | One month to notify the FDI Directorate of liquidation, bankruptcy or transformation |
| 12 | €50,000 administrative fine on the enterprise and the holder for breach of §2, and on the holder for breach of §10 or §11 |

**Implementing decision: KYA 216761 of 12 November 2025, ΦΕΚ Β΄ 6138 of 18
November 2025** — ministers of Foreign Affairs, Development, and Migration and
Asylum. Documents for realisation and for holding: registry certificate not
older than two months, proof of the securities, the bank remittance, the ERGANI
personnel table evidencing the two jobs, annual holding evidence, and a **€2,500
electronic fee**.

So the route was on the books from December 2024 and usable from November 2025.
Greek business reporting of the period describes it as live and barely used.

### What we had published

`article-en-greece-process.md` carried a section headed "The €250,000 startup
route that does not exist", an excerpt saying "There is no new 250,000 euro
golden visa for investing in startups", and an FAQ answering "No". The reasoning
was that the market's figures do not appear in article 79Α — which is true, and
was not the question. The market was describing a real permit and attributing it
to the wrong law.

### What the market actually gets wrong

Not the existence, and not the numbers. The attribution: page after page credits
the €250,000 startup route to Law 5275/2026 of February 2026, which neither
created nor amended it, and several give it article 79Α's name, "Tech Visa" —
which is an employment route with no investment threshold at all. Three separate
instruments, two of them fused and one of them misdated.

  jurisdictions sees one Greek investment route where there are two.

## A method note that cost thirteen collisions

The keyword-collision sweep had been run twice as a throwaway script keyed to
the heading `## Keywords`. Four articles head that block «Ключевые слова»,
«Słowa kluczowe», «Семантика» or «Semantyka». They were never swept, and a
skipped file looks exactly like a clean one. Thirteen collisions were hiding
there, including the hub page claiming four country-programme names in Russian
and Polish after the same claim had been taken off it in English.

The sweep is now `scripts/keywords.mjs`, `npm run keywords`, and an article
without a recognised block is an error rather than a silent skip. The general
rule: a checker that can skip its input must fail on the skip, not on the
finding.
