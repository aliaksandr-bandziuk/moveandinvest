## Article

Residence in Poland comes down to one question: "on what basis am I here, and what happens next?" That basis can be temporary, permanent or, eventually, citizenship, and each step has its own conditions, deadlines and traps. Most problems do not come from the complexity of the law but from a missed date: the last day of your lawful stay, the 14 days to appeal, the 15 working days to report the loss of a job.

This page is a map. Each section answers the main question briefly and points to a detailed page with references to the statutes and to [our sources page](/sources#pl-legal).

### Residence in Poland in 2026: the essentials in one minute

- **Temporary residence permit** (*zezwolenie na pobyt czasowy*) — a residence card (*karta pobytu*) for the duration of the purpose of your stay, **no longer than 3 years**; the application goes in **no later than the last day** of your lawful stay and, since 27 April 2026, **only through MOS**, the Office for Foreigners' online case system.
- **Fees** — **340 zł** for the permit, **440 zł** for a work-based permit and the EU Blue Card, plus **100 zł** for the card; for applications filed from 5 March 2026 the fee is not refunded after a refusal.
- **Deadlines at the voivode** (*wojewoda*) are suspended **until 4 March 2027** — for all foreigners.
- **Citizens of Ukraine** — UKR status, a **CUKR** card for 3 years if the application is filed by **4 March 2027**, or an ordinary permit on one of five grounds.
- **Permanently** — a permanent residence permit on specific grounds or **EU long-term resident** status after **5 years**, **640 zł** each.
- **Citizenship** — recognition as a Polish citizen by the voivode, the basic period being **3 years** on a permanent residence permit or EU long-term resident status, fee **1000 zł**.
- **Refusal** — **14 days** to appeal; after a final refusal, **30 days** to leave.

### How to apply for a residence card in Poland: MOS, documents and processing time

- **Filing** — electronically only, through **MOS**, signed with a trusted profile (*profil zaufany*) or a qualified electronic signature; after that you attend in person for fingerprints with your **original passport**.
- **At filing** you must attach the application, a photo, a scan of every page of your passport and, for a work-based permit, **annex no. 1** (*załącznik nr 1*) from your employer.
- **Other documents** — on the voivode's summons, with a deadline of no less than **14 days**; they must be valid **on the day the decision is issued**.
- **Processing time** — 60 days under the statute, but until 4 March 2027 deadlines at the voivode are suspended.
- **Case status** is checked with the voivode for your place of stay; there is no single service, and MOS does not show the status once the application is filed.

The grounds, conditions and fees for each type of card are in the detailed page on the [karta pobytu](entry:poland-karta-pobytu).

### Frequently asked questions about residence in Poland

? How can a foreigner legalise their stay in Poland?
Through a temporary residence permit on one of the grounds — work, business, study, marriage, family and others — and, for citizens of Ukraine, also through UKR status and the CUKR card. After that come permanent residence or EU long-term resident status, and citizenship.

? When should I apply for a residence card?
No later than the last day of your lawful stay. An application filed while you are staying unlawfully is a ground for refusal.

? How do I apply for a residence card in 2026?
Since 27 April 2026, electronically only, through MOS; then you attend for fingerprints with your original passport and hand in documents when the voivode summons you.

? How much does legalisation of stay in Poland cost?
The stamp duty (*opłata skarbowa*) for a temporary residence permit is 340 zł, for a work-based permit and the EU Blue Card 440 zł, plus 100 zł for the card; permanent residence and EU long-term resident status cost 640 zł each; recognition as a Polish citizen costs 1000 zł.

? How long does a residence card decision take?
Under the statute, 60 days; but until 4 March 2027 deadlines at the voivode are suspended for all foreigners, and a complaint about delay has no basis during that period.

? How do I check whether I am in Poland legally?
Look at the basis of your stay: a visa, visa-free travel, a residence card, UKR status or an application filed on time. The status of a case is checked with the voivode for your place of stay.

? What happens if my residence card is refused?
You have 14 days to appeal. Until the decision is final, your stay is lawful. After a final refusal you have 30 days to leave, and a new application filed within that period is not considered.

? What are the rules for citizens of Ukraine in 2026?
UKR status and the documents extended by statute to 4 March 2027, a CUKR card applied for by the same date, or an ordinary permit on five grounds.

? After how many years can I get permanent residence in Poland?
EU long-term resident status — after 5 years of lawful, continuous stay, with income, B1, insurance and housing. A permanent residence permit on specific grounds may require no period of stay at all.
