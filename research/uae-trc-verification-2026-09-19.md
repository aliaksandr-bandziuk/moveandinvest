## 1. Who is a UAE tax resident — Cabinet Decision No. 85 of 2022

Issued 2 September 2022, in force 1 March 2023. FTA unofficial translation,
`tax.gov.ae/Datafolder/Files/Legislation/Corporate Tax/Cabinet Decision 85 of 2022 - For publishing.pdf`, read 19 Sep 2026.

- **Art. 4** — a natural person is a Tax Resident if ANY of:
  1. the usual or primary place of residence AND the centre of financial and
     personal interests are in the State (or he meets conditions set by a
     Minister's decision);
  2. physically present **183 days or more** within the relevant **12
     consecutive months**;
  3. physically present **90 days or more** within the relevant 12 consecutive
     months AND is a UAE national, holds a valid Residence Permit, or is a GCC
     national, AND either (a) has a Permanent Place of Residence in the State or
     (b) carries on an employment or Business in the State.
- **Art. 1** — "Residence Permit" excludes temporary permits to enter for
  travel, tourism, sport, medical treatment etc.; "Tax Residency Certificate" =
  a certificate issued by the Authority proving that the Person is a Tax
  Resident in the State.
- **Art. 5** — a Tax Resident may apply to the Authority; form and manner set
  by the Authority; if satisfied, the Authority "may approve the application
  and issue" the certificate.
- **Art. 6** — where an International Agreement sets its own residence
  conditions, those apply for that agreement; the Minister sets the form of the
  certificate for treaty purposes.

## 2. How the tests are read — Ministerial Decision No. 27 of 2023

Issued 22 February 2023, in force 1 March 2023. FTA unofficial translation, read 19 Sep 2026.

- **Art. 2** — usual or primary place of residence = where the person
  habitually or normally resides, i.e. spends most of his time compared to any
  other jurisdiction as part of a settled routine, "more than transient";
  centre of financial and personal interests = where personal and economic
  interests are closest or of greatest significance — occupation, family and
  social relations, cultural and other activities, place of business, place
  from which property is administered.
- **Art. 3** — "day" = calendar day; every day or part of a day physically in
  the UAE counts; the days need not be consecutive.
- **Art. 4** — days spent in the UAE due to exceptional circumstances (beyond
  the person's control, arising while already in the UAE, unforeseeable,
  preventing departure as planned) MAY be disregarded by the Authority.
- **Art. 5** — Permanent Place of Residence = a furnished house, apartment,
  room or other dwelling continuously available; need not be owned — may be
  rented.
- **Art. 6** — employment = a contract with a UAE employer for remuneration
  paid in the State, or a continuing relationship where substantially all
  income for labour performed in the State comes from one party; full or part
  time; unpaid voluntary roles are not employment.

## 3. Certificates for treaty purposes — Ministerial Decision No. 247 of 2023

Issued 16 October 2023, in force from 1 March 2023. mof.gov.ae, read 19 Sep 2026.

- **Art. 2** — a person meeting the residence conditions of the relevant
  International Agreement may apply for a certificate for that agreement; the
  FTA issues it in the attached form or another form it accepts.
- **The attached form** names the treaty and the other country, and states
  "This certificate is valid from [..] to [..]".

## 4. The procedure — FTA guide TPGTR1 (October 2024) and the FTA service page (updated 11 August 2026)

Guide: "Tax Resident and Tax Residency Certificate — Tax Procedures Guide
TPGTR1", `tax.gov.ae/Datafolder/Files/Guides/VAT/VAT Guides/Tax-Resident-and-TRC--18-10-2024.pdf`;
the guide says of itself that it is not legally binding. Service page:
`tax.gov.ae/en/services/issuance.of.tax.certificates.aspx`, "last updated
Tuesday, August 11, 2026". Both read 19 Sep 2026.

- **Two kinds** of certificate: for a double taxation agreement (DTA), naming
  the treaty and country, and for other purposes (guide 7.1, 7.4).
- **Period** (guide 7.2; service page): the applicant selects a Tax Period —
  for a natural person, the Gregorian calendar year — or another 12-month
  period; the current or a prior period only, never a future one, and never
  longer than 12 months. For the current period a natural person may apply
  "as soon as the criteria to be Tax Resident are met".
- **Channel** (guide 7.3): EmaraTax, "other services" → "Tax Residency
  Certificate"; a person with no Corporate Tax TRN chooses "No TRN"; select
  the DTA type and country or the other type; upload documents; pay the
  submission fee; submit; after approval pay the processing fee within 30
  business days or the application may be cancelled; download the digital
  certificate, which is also emailed; a printed copy is couriered by Emirates
  Post on request.
- **Fees** (guide 7.7, citing Cabinet Decision No. 65 of 2020; service page
  identical on 11 Aug 2026): submission AED 50, non-refundable; processing
  after approval AED 500 for a registrant with a Corporate Tax TRN, **AED 1,000
  for a natural person not registered with the FTA**, AED 1,750 for a juridical
  person not registered; AED 250 per printed copy. Cabinet Decision 65/2020 was
  not read; the amounts are as the FTA publishes them.
- **Time** (guide 7.6; service page): generally 10 business days from a
  complete application; the answer is approval, rejection or a request for
  more information, to which the applicant has 30 business days; printed copy
  5 business days after payment, delivered in the UAE only.
- **Withdrawal** (guide 7.6): the FTA may withdraw a certificate on learning of
  incorrect information or a change of facts.
- **Documents for a natural person, non-treaty certificate** (guide 7.5.1):
  - 183 days or more: Emirates ID and resident visa, OR passport copy plus an
    entry and exit report from the Federal Authority for Identity and
    Citizenship (ICP) or a local competent government entity;
  - 90–182 days: the same identity evidence AND either proof of employment or
    Business (source of income, salary certificate, evidence of business) or
    proof of a permanent place of residence (certified tenancy contract, other
    long-term rental contract, landlord's written statement, or title deed
    with a utility bill in the person's name);
  - primary residence and centre of interests: the identity evidence AND a
    written statement explaining why the financial and personal interests are
    in the UAE with supporting documents AND proof of primary residence AND,
    if applicable, proof of income (salary certificate, share certificate,
    trade licence, savings, local bank statements).
- **Documents for a treaty certificate** (guide 7.5.2): the identity evidence,
  proof of residence if applicable, source of income / salary certificate; if
  the treaty's own residence rule refers to being resident in the UAE, the
  documents of the non-treaty table.
- **Foreign forms** (guide 8): the FTA can, on request, stamp another
  country's own residence form covering the same 12 months and country as the
  certificate; the form must be in English or Arabic or translated; included
  in the processing fee; generally 10 business days; can also be uploaded with
  the application to be returned signed, if the other country accepts it.

## Not established, and not to be stated on the page

- Which countries accept which kind of certificate, and any country's own rule
  for a person leaving it (e.g. Russia's or Ukraine's residence tests) — the
  page tells the reader to ask the tax authority of the country that wants the
  certificate.
- The number of UAE double tax treaties.
- Processing times in practice beyond the FTA's "generally 10 business days".
- Anything about the Arabic original text differing from the unofficial
  translation.
