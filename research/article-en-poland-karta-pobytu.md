## Article

A **residence card** (*karta pobytu*) is not the permit itself but the document that confirms it. The same plastic card can stand for different permits: temporary residence, permanent residence, EU long-term resident status, or the CUKR card permit for citizens of Ukraine. When people say "getting a TRC" or "getting a karta pobytu", they almost always mean the first — a **temporary residence permit** (*zezwolenie na pobyt czasowy*).

A temporary residence permit is issued by the **voivode (*wojewoda*) for the place where you are staying**, for the period the purpose requires, but **no longer than 3 years**. The ground falls into one of seven groups: work, the EU Blue Card, business, study, marriage to a Polish citizen, family reunification and other circumstances. Each has its own conditions, and the ground decides whether you may work for anyone or only for the employer named in the decision.

Below are all the grounds, with the articles of the Act on Foreigners (*ustawa o cudzoziemcach*), the conditions for each, filing through MOS, the fees, the time limits and the way on to permanent residence and citizenship. Every rule was compared with the text of the act; the links lead to the [sources page](/sources#pl-legal) with the date we read it. All the routes to legal stay, and what to do at each stage, are on the overview page [Legalisation of stay in Poland](entry:poland-legalisation-pillar).

**A note on "work permit Poland".** This page covers the combined **temporary residence and work permit** (*zezwolenie na pobyt czasowy i pracę*), which a foreigner applies for to the voivode. The separate work permit (*zezwolenie na pracę*, type A) that an employer obtains is a different procedure and is not covered here.

### Residence permit in Poland in 2026: the short version

- **What it is.** The document for a permit to stay. A temporary card is valid for as long as the permit.
- **Length of the permit.** No longer than 3 years, depending on the purpose of stay.
- **When to apply.** No later than the last day of your legal stay. An application filed during an illegal stay is a ground for refusal.
- **How to apply.** Since 27 April 2026 only electronically through MOS, followed by an appointment to give fingerprints.
- **Work.** Pay no lower than the minimum wage — 4806 zł in 2026 — whatever the working time.
- **Without work.** 30 days after losing the job, if you notified the voivode within 15 working days, and only once during the permit.
- **Cost.** 340 zł for the permit, 440 zł for work and the Blue Card, plus 100 zł for the card. For applications filed from 5 March 2026 the fee is not refunded after a refusal.
- **Processing time.** 60 days under the act, but time limits at the voivode are suspended until 4 March 2027.

### Temporary residence permit in Poland: grounds and conditions in one table

A temporary residence permit is granted if the foreigner meets the requirements of their purpose and that purpose justifies a stay longer than 3 months, art. 98. The main grounds:

| Ground | Article | Main conditions | Free access to the labour market |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Work | 114 | Insurance; pay no lower than comparable workers' and no lower than the minimum wage; an occupation not on the restrictive list | No, work as set in the decision |
| EU Blue Card | 127 | A contract of at least 6 months, higher professional qualifications, insurance | No, work as set in the decision |
| Business | 142 | Insurance, stable income, housing; a company with revenue or employees | No |
| University study | 144 | Admission, payment for a semester or year, insurance, means to live on | Yes |
| Marriage to a Polish citizen | 158 | A marriage recognised by Polish law | Yes |
| Family reunification | 159 | The sponsor's status, insurance, income above the threshold, housing | Yes |
| Other circumstances | 186, 187 | Depends on the case: graduate of a Polish university, EU resident of another country, family life and others | Varies |

The last column follows art. 3 of the [Act on the conditions for entrusting work to foreigners](/sources#pl-legal-praca-cudzoziemcow); more on it below.

### Temporary residence and work permit and the EU Blue Card in Poland: conditions and minimum wage

The temporary residence and work permit is the most common ground. Art. 114 requires all of the following at once:

1. **Health insurance** in Poland, or confirmation that an insurer covers treatment. The condition counts as met if the insurance arises together with this job.
2. **An occupation not on the list** that may restrict access under the Act on the conditions for entrusting work to foreigners.
3. **Pay no lower than Polish workers'** in a comparable position and with the same working time.
4. **Pay no lower than the minimum wage — whatever the working time and type of contract.** In 2026 that is **4806 zł** a month, under the [Council of Ministers regulation](/sources#pl-legal-placa-minimalna-2026). If you work for several employers, the total counts.

Point 4 is the main trap: a half-time job paying less than 4806 zł will not support a work-based card, even if such a contract is lawful under employment law.

**The employer is checked too.** The permit will not be granted if the employer does not carry on business, has suspended it or is winding it up, does not pay contributions or taxes, has been penalised for illegal employment, or lacks the funds to pay you, arts. 117 and 117a.

**What the decision states.** The employer, the position, the minimum pay, the working time and the type of contract, art. 118. This is what "tied to the employer" means: you may work only on those terms.

**The EU Blue Card** (*Niebieska Karta UE*), art. 127, is for work requiring high qualifications: a contract of at least 6 months, higher professional qualifications, insurance, and annual pay no lower than the threshold set by law. The fee is the same as for the work-based permit.

### Changing employer on a TRC in Poland, and how long you may be without work

**When the permit does not need changing**, art. 119:

- the employer's address, name or legal form has changed;
- the business has passed to another employer;
- your hours have gone up with a proportional rise in pay;
- the job title has changed without new duties;
- a civil-law contract has been replaced by an employment contract.

In these cases the employer notifies the voivode within 15 working days.

**In every other case** — a new employer, a different position, lower pay or fewer hours — you must **change the permit**, art. 120. You can apply at any time, and the permit's validity stays the same. The application to change it is filed **on paper**, not through MOS, art. 120a; the fee is 220 zł. How to go through this step by step, and what to do if the job is already gone, is covered in the entry on changing employer.

**If you have lost your job:**

| What | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| Notify the voivode who granted the permit | Within **15 working days**, art. 121 |
| An application to change the permit filed within that time | Counts as the notification, art. 121(3) |
| How long you may be without work | **30 days** from losing the job, art. 123 |
| How many times | If you lose work with all employers — **once** during the permit |
| Occupations desirable for the economy (art. 114(1a)) | Up to **3 months** without work, no more than **twice** |

Without the notification there is no grace period: the permit may be withdrawn because the purpose of stay has ended, art. 101. It is also withdrawn when your pay was cut or your position changed without changing the permit, art. 122.

### A residence card not tied to an employer: who can get one

Legally, "not tied" means **free access to the labour market**: you may work for any employer without a work permit. Not every temporary permit gives it. Under art. 3(1) of the Act on the conditions for entrusting work to foreigners, free access is held by holders of a temporary residence permit:

- **for university study**, art. 144, and for scientific research;
- **as the spouse of a Polish citizen**, granted in connection with the marriage, and for that spouse's children under 21 or dependent;
- **for family reunification**, art. 159;
- after divorce from, or the death of, a Polish citizen spouse, art. 158(2);
- as an EU long-term resident of another country and their family members, art. 186(1)(3)–(4), and in several other cases.

**The second route is exemption from the work permit**, art. 3(5). If you are in Poland legally on a temporary residence permit, you do not need a work permit when you have:

- a valid **Pole's Card** (*Karta Polaka*);
- a **degree from a Polish university**;
- a Polish doctorate or a place in a Polish doctoral school;
- a Polish secondary school certificate together with Polish documents confirming vocational qualifications.

For these people the residence and work permit **does not name an employer**; it states the ground for the exemption instead, arts. 114(4) and 118(3)–(4) of the Act on Foreigners. This is what intermediaries most often sell as a "card not tied to an employer".

**A business-based card does not give free access**: art. 142(1) is not on the list in art. 3. **A work-based card** does not either — you may work only as set in the decision.

**A "card not tied to an employer" cannot be bought.** The ground either exists — marriage, family, study, the Pole's Card, a Polish degree — or it does not. After 5 years of legal stay, free access comes with [EU long-term resident status](entry:poland-permanent).

### Residence card through marriage to a Polish citizen, and family reunification

**Marriage to a Polish citizen**, art. 158. You need a marriage recognised by Polish law. The article itself sets no conditions on income, insurance or housing. The spouse's minor child also receives a permit. After divorce or separation a further permit is granted if your important interest supports it; after the spouse's death, without that condition — in both cases once and for no longer than 3 years. After 3 years of marriage and 2 years on such a permit, [permanent residence](entry:poland-permanent) opens up.

**Family reunification**, art. 159, is for the spouse and minor children of a foreigner who lives in Poland:

| Condition | What the law requires |
| --- | --- |
| The sponsor's status | Among others: permanent residence, EU long-term resident, refugee, subsidiary protection, Blue Card, **or 2 years** on consecutive temporary permits, the last one for at least a year |
| Insurance | Health insurance in Poland |
| Income | Stable, above the social assistance threshold for each family member |
| Housing | Secured accommodation |

**The income threshold** is the eligibility threshold for social assistance, art. 163 read with art. 140(2), under the [Council of Ministers regulation](/sources#pl-legal-kryteria-dochodowe) in force from 1 January 2025: **1010 zł** for a person living alone and **823 zł** per person in a family.

### Temporary residence permit for business, study and other circumstances

**Business**, art. 142. For the foreigner: insurance, stable and regular income, housing. For the company, one of three:

- revenue for the previous tax year of **at least 12 times the average monthly wage** in the voivodeship where it is registered;
- **2 employees** full-time on open-ended contracts for at least a year before the application;
- funds or activities that will allow one of these conditions to be met in future: investment, technology, jobs.

A permit is also granted to a management board member of your own sp. z o.o. or S.A., a general partner and a commercial proxy (*prokurent*), art. 142(3).

**Study**, art. 144: admission to a university approved to accept foreigners, payment for a semester or a year where the course is paid, insurance, and means to live on and to travel home.

**Other circumstances**, arts. 186 and 187. The most practical cases:

- **a graduate of a Polish university** looking for work or starting a business: degree, insurance, housing, means;
- **an EU long-term resident of another country** who works, studies or lives in Poland;
- **family life** under the European Convention on Human Rights, or the rights of a child — the voivode **may** grant these permits even to a person staying in Poland illegally, art. 187(6)–(7).

### Residence card for citizens of Ukraine: UKR, CUKR and the five grounds

If you are a citizen of Ukraine with UKR status, you have three routes:

1. **Stay on UKR status.** Your stay is legal while the EU Council decision on temporary protection is in force; according to the Office for Foreigners (*UdSC*) it has been extended until 4 March 2028.
2. **Apply for the CUKR card** — a three-year permit not tied to an employer. The application must be filed by 4 March 2027.
3. **Apply for an ordinary temporary residence permit.** There is a restriction here.

**If you already have a residence card**, not UKR status: for citizens of Ukraine, the validity of temporary permits and cards that was expiring from 24 February 2022 onwards **was extended by law until 4 March 2027** — with no application and no replacement, art. 42 of the Act on assistance to citizens of Ukraine. You cannot cross the border on a card extended this way. What to do before 4 March 2027 is covered in the entry on renewing a residence card.

**The restriction on grounds.** The Act on Foreigners refuses to open proceedings for a person under temporary protection, art. 99(1)(4). The exception for UKR status covers only **five grounds**, art. 45 of the [Act Dz.U. 2026 poz. 203](/sources#pl-legal-ustawa-2026-203):

| You may apply with UKR status | Article of the Act on Foreigners |
| --- | --- |
| Residence and work | 114(1) |
| EU Blue Card | 127 |
| Business | 142(1) and (3) |
| Spouse of a Polish citizen | 158(1) |
| Family reunification, without the sponsor's two-year minimum | 159(1) |

For study and other circumstances we found no such exception in the law — on our reading of the two provisions, you cannot apply on those grounds with UKR status.

**UKR status ends on the day the temporary residence permit is granted**, art. 109b of the Act on granting protection to foreigners. So plan the move from UKR to a work-based card with a margin: a work permit rests on one employer, and CUKR does not.

### How to apply for a TRC in Poland: MOS step by step

Since 27 April 2026 an application for a temporary residence permit is filed **only electronically through MOS** (*Moduł Obsługi Spraw*), the Office for Foreigners' online case system. An application filed any other way is left unexamined, art. 106c of the [amendment introducing MOS](/sources#pl-legal-nowelizacja-mos).

1. **Apply in time** — no later than the last day of your legal stay, art. 105. The last day of your visa, visa-free stay or previous permit is the last day to apply.
2. **Fill in the form in MOS** and attach a current photo and scans of **every page** of a valid passport, art. 106d.
3. **The employer's or university's annex** is completed by them: MOS sends them a link to the email address given in the application, and they sign the annex with their own signature.
4. **Supporting documents** — contract, insurance, proof of income, a document on your housing — can be attached at once as scans.
5. **Sign** the application with a qualified electronic signature or a trusted profile (*profil zaufany*). The application counts as filed once it is signed and an official confirmation of receipt has arrived.
6. **Get the certificate** (*zaświadczenie*) of filing through MOS. While the case is pending, your stay is legal until the decision becomes final, art. 108.
7. **Attend when summoned** — no earlier than 7 days after the summons is delivered — with your passport, to give fingerprints and a specimen signature, art. 106e.
8. **Present documents** when summoned, within a time limit of at least 14 days, art. 106f — including the originals of those you attached as scans.

**The main risk is step 7.** If you do not attend on a proper summons, do not give fingerprints or a signature, or do not show your passport, **the proceedings are discontinued**, art. 106i. Summonses do not arrive in MOS but through e-Doręczenia (electronic delivery) or by registered letter, and a letter not collected is treated as delivered after 14 days — see the entry on checking your case status.

Children under 6 give no fingerprints and need not attend; under 13 they give no signature. The voivode's office is obliged to help you file the application.

### How much a Poland residence card costs

| What | Stamp duty (*opłata skarbowa*) |
| --- | --- |
| Temporary residence, general case | **340 zł** |
| Residence and work, including desirable occupations | **440 zł** |
| EU Blue Card | **440 zł** |
| Change of a residence and work permit | **220 zł** |
| Issuing the card | **100 zł**, a separate payment to the voivode's office |

Amounts and exemptions follow the annex to the [Stamp Duty Act](/sources#pl-legal-oplata-skarbowa); the card fee follows the [MOS explanation](/sources#pl-legal-mos-praca-dokumenty). Exempt from the temporary residence fee are, among others, victims of human trafficking and several other categories.

**For applications filed from 5 March 2026 the fee is not refunded** if the voivode refuses the permit or discontinues the proceedings, art. 113c of the Act on Foreigners; the same applies to changing a work-based permit, art. 120c. So missing the fingerprint appointment costs not only the case but also 340 or 440 zł.

### TRC processing time in Poland

Under the act, a decision on temporary residence is issued within **60 days** — counted from a complete application, or from the documents the voivode requested, art. 112a.

But at the voivode **time limits in residence cases are suspended until 4 March 2027**, art. 100d of the Act on assistance to citizens of Ukraine. This applies to all foreigners, not only Ukrainians, and a complaint about delay has no basis during this period. How to check your case status and what to do if it has stalled is covered in a separate entry.

### What a residence card gives you: travel, work, further status

- **Legal stay** in Poland for the length of the permit.
- **Crossing the border without a visa**, together with your passport, art. 242.
- **Travel in the Schengen area.** According to the [Podlaskie Voivode's Office](/sources#pl-legal-puw-podlaski-podroze) — up to 90 days in any 180, with a valid passport and sufficient means.
- **Work** — freely or as set in the decision, depending on the ground.
- **A path to permanent residence.** Years on temporary permits count towards the 5 years needed for EU long-term resident status.
- **Buying a home.** A foreigner buys a flat without a permit; for a house with land, a temporary residence permit is one of the ties to Poland needed for a permit from the Ministry of the Interior (MSWiA).
- **Driving licence.** A foreign licence is valid in Poland for 6 months from the start of your stay; after that it is exchanged for a Polish one.

**The filing certificate (*zaświadczenie*) is not a card.** It confirms legal stay while the case is pending, but it gives no right to cross the border: you can leave, but you cannot come back on it — you need your own visa or visa-free entry.

### Refusal and loss of a residence permit

**Proceedings will not be opened** if on the day you apply you already hold a permanent residence permit or EU long-term resident status, you are under temporary protection (except the five grounds for UKR), or you are outside Poland, art. 99.

**The permit will be refused**, art. 100, if among other things:

- the conditions of the purpose are not met;
- you are on the list of foreigners whose stay is undesirable, or in the SIS;
- the application contains false information or documents;
- you have **tax arrears**;
- the application was filed **during an illegal stay**.

**The permit is withdrawn** if the purpose of stay has ended or the conditions have stopped being met, art. 101. So withdrawal can follow from a job lost for longer than the 30 days of grace, a divorce on a marriage-based permit, or being struck off a university's roll on a study permit. If a refusal is already final or the card has expired, every day counts — what to do in each of these situations is set out in the entry on deportation from Poland.

An appeal against a refusal is filed within **14 days** of the decision being delivered, through the voivode, to the Head of the Office for Foreigners (*Szef Urzędu do Spraw Cudzoziemców*). What to write in the appeal, how long it takes and what happens after a final refusal is covered in the entry on a refused residence card.

### Frequently asked questions about the Poland residence card

? What is a residence card (karta pobytu)?
The document that confirms a permit to stay in Poland: temporary, permanent, EU long-term resident, or under the CUKR card. With your passport, the card lets you cross the border without a visa. In everyday speech "karta pobytu" or "TRC" usually means a temporary residence permit for up to 3 years.

? What types of residence card are there in Poland?
Behind the card there can be a temporary residence permit (up to 3 years), the CUKR card for citizens of Ukraine (3 years), a permanent residence permit (a card for 10 years) or EU long-term resident status (a card for 5 years).

? How do I get a temporary residence permit in Poland?
Choose the ground — work, business, study, marriage, family or other circumstances — file the application through MOS no later than the last day of your legal stay, attend when summoned to give fingerprints, and present the documents. The decision is taken by the voivode for the place where you are staying.

? What salary do I need for a work-based residence card?
No lower than comparable workers' pay, and no lower than the minimum wage whatever your working time. In 2026 the minimum wage is 4806 zł a month; with several employers the total counts.

? How long can I be without work on a residence card?
30 days from losing the job, if you notified the voivode within 15 working days. If you lose work with all your employers, this applies once during the permit. For occupations desirable for the economy — up to 3 months, no more than twice.

? Can I get a residence card not tied to an employer?
Yes, if you have a ground with free access to the labour market: marriage to a Polish citizen, family reunification, university study, some other circumstances. Holders of the Pole's Card or a Polish university degree who are in Poland legally on a temporary residence permit also work without a work permit. Such a ground cannot be bought.

? How do I know whether my residence card is tied to an employer?
Look at the voivode's decision: if it names the employer, the position and the pay, you may work only on those terms. If instead of an employer it states a ground for exemption from the work permit, there is no tie.

? Can I apply for a residence card with UKR status?
Yes, but only on five grounds: work, the Blue Card, business, marriage to a Polish citizen and family reunification. UKR status ends on the day the permit is granted. The alternative is the CUKR card, with the application due by 4 March 2027.

? How much does a residence card cost in Poland?
Stamp duty of 340 zł for the permit, 440 zł for work and the Blue Card, 220 zł for changing a work-based permit, plus 100 zł for issuing the card. For applications from 5 March 2026 the fee is not refunded after a refusal or discontinuance.

? How long is the waiting time for a TRC in Poland?
60 days under the act, but time limits in residence cases at the voivode are suspended until 4 March 2027, so there is currently no legal deadline. While the case is pending, your stay is legal.

? How much money do I need in my account for a residence card?
It depends on the ground. For work, the salary matters; for family and business, a stable income above the social assistance threshold: 1010 zł for a person living alone and 823 zł per family member. For study, means to live on and travel home above the same threshold.

? Can I leave Poland with a residence card?
Yes. With your passport, the card lets you cross the border without a visa, and you can travel in the Schengen area for up to 90 days in any 180. Long absences carry a different risk: a permit is withdrawn if the purpose of stay has ended, and they can interrupt the 5 years needed for EU resident status.

? Can I leave Poland while waiting for a residence permit?
You can leave, but the filing certificate (*zaświadczenie*) is not a card and gives no right to come back: to return you need your own visa or visa-free entry.

? What happens if I apply for a residence card after my visa has expired?
An application filed during an illegal stay is a ground for refusal, art. 100. You must apply no later than the last day of your legal stay.

? How long must I live in Poland to get a residence card?
There is no minimum period for a temporary residence permit — what matters is the ground and legal stay on the day you apply. Minimum periods apply to the sponsor in family reunification — 2 years — and to the next steps: 5 years for EU long-term resident status.

### When a residence card is not a simple case

Filing for a work-based card yourself is realistic if the employer is reliable, the pay is above 4806 zł, you have the documents in hand and you can file before your legal stay ends. It is different if:

- your legal stay ends in a few days;
- you are on UKR status and choosing between CUKR and a work-based card;
- your employer changed or you lost your job while the case was pending;
- the ground is marriage or family and there are questions about documents from abroad;
- a refusal or withdrawal has arrived, or the case was discontinued for failure to attend.
