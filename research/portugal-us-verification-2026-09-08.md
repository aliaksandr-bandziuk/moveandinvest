## 1. The naturalisation clock

**Nothing new was read for this section.** It rests entirely on
`portugal-verification-2026-08-28.md`, re-read at the official PDF on
7 September, and on `article-en-portugal-citizenship.md`. Repeated here only so
the D1 article has one file to check itself against.

**Art. 15.º of the Nationality Act (Lei n.º 37/81), current text, n.os 1, 3 and 4:**

> "1 — Para os efeitos do disposto nos artigos precedentes, entende-se que
> residem legalmente no território português os indivíduos que aqui se
> encontram, com a sua situação regularizada perante as autoridades portuguesas,
> ao abrigo de qualquer dos títulos, vistos ou autorizações previstos no regime
> de entrada, permanência, saída e afastamento de estrangeiros e no regime do
> direito de asilo.
>
> 3 — Para os efeitos de contagem de prazos de residência legal previstos na
> presente lei, considera-se a soma de todos os períodos de residência legal em
> território nacional, seguidos ou interpolados, desde que os mesmos tenham
> decorrido num intervalo máximo de 6, 9 ou 12 anos, consoante os interessados
> sejam apátridas, cidadãos nacionais de países de língua oficial portuguesa e de
> Estados-Membros da União Europeia, ou cidadãos de outros países.
>
> 4 — [Revogado.]"

**The repealed n.º 4, as inserted by Lei Orgânica 1/2024 of 5 March 2024:**

> "Para os efeitos de contagem de prazos de residência legal previstos na
> presente lei, considera-se igualmente o tempo decorrido desde o momento em que
> foi requerida a autorização de residência temporária, desde que a mesma venha a
> ser deferida."

**The repealing provision, Lei Orgânica 1/2026, art. 5.º:**

> "São revogados os n.os 5, 7 e 13 do artigo 6.º, as alíneas b) e d) do n.º 1 e
> os n.os 3 e 4 do artigo 9.º, o n.º 4 do artigo 12.º-B, o artigo 13.º e o n.º 4
> do artigo 15.º da Lei n.º 37/81"

**Art. 8.º:** in force the day after publication — **19 May 2026**.
**Art. 7.º n.º 2:** procedures pending on that date keep the previous text,
n.º 4 included.

**WHAT MUST NOT BE WRITTEN.** Not "the period runs from the issue of the
residence permit". That is a paraphrase of a repeal rather than a rule the
statute states, and it is wrong for pending files. Write the repeal, and write
both regimes.

**Period for an American:** ten years (art. 6.º n.º 1 as amended), with the
twelve-year window of art. 15.º n.º 3 for interrupted periods — "cidadãos de
outros países" is the third limb of that sentence.

## 2. The income tax convention

Read 8 September 2026 at two independent official copies, because the finding
below is structural and a single source would not be enough:

- IRS: https://www.irs.gov/pub/irs-trty/portugal.pdf
- US Treasury: https://home.treasury.gov/system/files/131/Treaty-Portugal-9-6-1994.pdf

**Signed** "this sixth day of September, 1994" at "Washington".
**Effective** from 1 January 1996; art. 30 n.º 2 provides that it enters into
force on the exchange of instruments of ratification, and the operative
provisions apply "for amounts paid or credited on or after the first day of
January next following the date on which the Convention enters into force".

**Article 1 (General Scope), in full — this is the whole article:**

> "This Convention shall apply to persons who are residents of one or both of
> the Contracting States, except as otherwise provided in the Convention."

**The saving clause is NOT in Article 1. It is in the Protocol, paragraph 1(b):**

> "Notwithstanding any provision of the Convention except paragraph (c) of this
> provision, a Contracting State may tax its residents (as determined under
> Article 4 (Residence)), and the United States may tax its citizens, as if the
> Convention had not come into effect."

Paragraph 1(c) lists the exceptions to it.

**WHY THIS IS THE FINDING.** In most US treaties the saving clause is a
paragraph of Article 1, so a reader who opens Article 1, sees a single sentence
about personal scope and stops has read the article that in other treaties
carries the clause — and has not found it. It sits in the Protocol, which is a
separate instrument printed after the signature block.

**WHAT MUST NOT BE WRITTEN.** Not "Article 1(3)", the numbering this clause
carries in other conventions. Not "the treaty stops double taxation" — it
allocates and relieves; the clause preserves US taxation of US citizens.

## 3. The totalization agreement

Read 8 September 2026 at ssa.gov.

- https://www.ssa.gov/international/status.html
- https://www.ssa.gov/international/Agreement_Pamphlets/portugal.html

**Table entry, verbatim:** "Agreement on Social Security | March 30, 1988 |
Aug. 1, 1989 | TIAS 12121" — signed 30 March 1988, **in force 1 August 1989**.

**Self-employment rule, verbatim:**

> "Self-employed workers who reside in Portugal are assigned Portuguese
> coverage."

**Certificate of coverage:** **form P/USA 1**, requested from "the regional
center of the social security system in Portugal where you are registered".

Note the symmetry with Greece, where the same instrument produces form
**GR/USA 1** — see `greece-citizenship-verification-2026-09-07.md` and the
`living-in-greece-as-an-american` article. The naming pattern is the agreement's,
not a coincidence, and neither form number may be inferred for a third country
without reading that country's pamphlet.

  never quoted with figures.
- **IFICI / "NHR 2.0" conditions.** Not read for this article. Get Golden Visa

  nothing about the regime here beyond that it exists and is not the subject.
