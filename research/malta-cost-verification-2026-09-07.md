# Malta, cost of living: verification, 7 September 2026

Read on 7 September 2026. Source status is marked on every item. Nothing here is
older than its stated date, and two of the most useful facts are absences.

## 1. THE FIRST FINDING IS A HOLE: Malta has no current household budget survey

**NSO Malta's own page, "The Household Budget Survey (HBS) explained":** the
previous HBS was conducted in **2015–2016**. The current one went into the field
in **November 2024** and ran to **December 2025**, over a full 52-week period.
Validation and analysis are "finalised by the third quarter of 2026", with
results "published before the end of 2026".

OFFICIAL, nso.gov.mt. https://nso.gov.mt/the-household-budget-survey-hbs-explained/
and https://nso.gov.mt/hbs/

**So the newest published official picture of what a Maltese household spends is
a decade old**, and the new one is due within months of this page's publication.
Every monthly-budget figure on the first results screen therefore rests on
something other than a national expenditure survey — and none of them says so.

**This is a form-B negative claim and the structure opened is named:** NSO's HBS
theme page, its HBS explainer, and its news-release index. It is not a claim that
no Maltese price data exists — the RPI and HICP are monthly and current, see §4.
It is a claim about the household expenditure survey specifically.

## 2. Rent: there IS official data, and it disagrees with the advertisements

Private residential leases have had to be registered with the **Housing
Authority since 1 January 2020**, so Malta has something Portugal only got
recently and most countries do not have at all: a register of what tenants
actually signed, as against what landlords asked.

**Housing Authority, Rent Report 2023 H2 update** (OFFICIAL, housingauthority.gov.mt):

- **60,339** active registered contracts at the end of 2023.
- Median rent, St Paul's Bay: **€750** for a two-bedroom, **€900** for a
  three-bedroom (Figures 12–13).
- St Paul's Bay alone holds just under **8,900** contracts — **15%** of the
  national register. **43%** of all contracts are in the Northern Harbour region,
  **24%** in the Northern region.
- Multi-year contracts: **median duration 4 years, mean 4.3 years**.

https://housingauthority.gov.mt/wp-content/uploads/2024/05/Rent-Report-2023-H2-Update-Housing-Authority.pdf

**THE NUMBER THAT MAKES THE PAGE. Central Bank of Malta, Working Paper WP/4/2025,
Sarah Spiteri, "Where Does Malta's P2P Rental Market Fit? A Case Study for 2023"**
(OFFICIAL, centralbankmalta.org):

- Median registered rent for **new contracts in 2023: €850**.
- Median on **Facebook Marketplace: €1,400**.
- Median in the **aggregated estate-agency dataset: €1,500**.
- The agency dataset skews large: **53%** of its listings have three or more
  bedrooms against **44%** in the official register.

Advertised medians are therefore **65% to 76% above** the registered median for
new contracts, and part of that gap is composition rather than price — the
listings are for bigger flats.

https://www.centralbankmalta.org/site/Publications/Economic%20Research/2025/WP-04-2025.pdf

**The same gap, measured three years earlier. Central Bank of Malta, Research
Bulletin 2022, Brian Micallef and Tiziana M. Gauci, "A comparison between
registered rents and advertised rents in Malta"** (OFFICIAL):

- 2021: **51%** of registered properties rented for **under €700 a month**,
  against **under 9%** of advertised ones.
- To 2022Q2, annual growth: **registered 4.7–5.7%**, **advertised 13.6%**.
- Registered data from the Housing Authority register (2020Q1–2022Q2); advertised
  from the Bank's own database of estate-agent listings (2017Q4–2022Q2).
- The Bank's own caution: renewed contracts, whose rent usually does not change,
  partly explain the slower growth in the registered series.

https://www.centralbankmalta.org/site/Reports-Articles/2022/Registered-Rents.pdf

**WHAT MUST NOT BE WRITTEN.** The newest *published* register median we have is
**2023**. Do not present €850 as the 2026 figure. The honest sentence is that the
newest official median for a new contract is 2023's €850, and that the advertised
figures the market quotes are for a different and larger set of properties.

## 3. What the register does not tell you

The register covers **private residential leases**. It does not price the
purchase market, does not cover commercial or tourism lets, and — since
registration is a legal duty rather than a survey — it describes contracts, not
households. A household's total outgoings cannot be read from it. That is what
the HBS is for, and see §1.

## 4. Prices are current even though expenditure is not

**NSO Malta, Harmonised Index of Consumer Prices, March 2026**, released
**16 April 2026** (OFFICIAL):

- Annual inflation **2.3%**, unchanged on the previous month.
- Twelve-month moving average **2.5%**.
- Largest upward contributions: recreation, sport and culture **4.6%**;
  restaurants and accommodation services **3.9%**.
- Largest downward: clothing and footwear **−3.9%**; information and
  communication **−3.6%**.

https://nso.gov.mt/harmonised-index-of-consumer-prices-hicp-march-2026/

NSO also publishes the Retail Price Index monthly. So Malta measures prices every
month and household spending once a decade — worth saying plainly, because it
explains why the honest answer to "what does it cost to live here" is partly a
refusal.

## 5. NOT VERIFIED — do not print

- **The Maltese national minimum wage for 2026.** legislation.mt is
  JavaScript-gated and S.L. 452.71 could not be read; every figure in circulation
  (including €11,931 gross a year, seen in a Facebook comment on the results
  page) is secondary. **Not printed until the National Standard Order or the
  amending Legal Notice is read.**
- **Median or average earnings.** NSO's SILC and Labour Force Survey were not
  opened this pass.
- **Any 2024, 2025 or 2026 median from the rent register.** Only the 2023 H2
  report was found published.
